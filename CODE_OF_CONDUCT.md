# Code of conduct

Craft Panel is made for kids and for people who are new to running a server, so this space has to feel safe for them too.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people asking basic questions. Everyone starts somewhere.
- Assume good intent, and explain instead of mocking.
- Keep discussions about the panel, Minecraft servers and how to make both easier.
- Respect privacy: never post someone's address, real name, IP, account details or private messages.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or discriminatory jokes or language of any kind.
- Sexual content, or any content that is not appropriate for minors.
- Spam, advertising, or links to cheats, cracked clients or malware.
- Asking anyone, especially a minor, to move the conversation to a private channel.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules, with or without a warning depending on how serious it is.

To report something, use **Report content** in the comment's `...` menu (it goes to GitHub), or reach the maintainers privately through the **Security → Report a vulnerability** form described in [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md). Reports are kept confidential.
